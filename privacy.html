Roomie Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Roomie collects, uses, and stores information when you use the Roomie mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Roomie collects information that you provide when creating an account and using the application.

This may include:

  • Email address and account information
  • Property information
  • Unit information
  • Tenant names and contact information
  • Rental and payment-related information entered by the user
  • Property and unit photos uploaded by the user

2. How We Use Information

Information collected through Roomie is used to provide the application's property and tenant management functionality.

The information may be used to:

  • Create and manage your Roomie account
  • Store and display your properties and units
  • Manage tenant information
  • Maintain rental and payment-related information
  • Store photos associated with properties or units
  • Provide and maintain the functionality of the application

3. Data Storage and Service Providers

Roomie uses Supabase as a backend service to provide authentication, database, and storage functionality.

Information you provide through Roomie may therefore be processed and stored using Supabase infrastructure as necessary to provide the application's functionality.

4. Analytics and Advertising

Roomie does not currently use third-party advertising services or analytics services to track users for advertising purposes.

Roomie does not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

5. Location Information

Roomie does not currently collect or use precise or approximate device location information.

6. Notifications

Roomie does not currently use push notifications.

7. Data Sharing

Roomie does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers, such as Supabase, where necessary to provide the application's backend, authentication, database, and storage functionality.

8. Account and Data Deletion

Roomie provides users with the ability to delete their account from within the application.

When requesting account deletion, associated account data will be handled according to Roomie's account deletion process and applicable legal or operational requirements.

9. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed through Roomie. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Roomie is a property management application and is not specifically designed or directed toward children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Roomie's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with an updated effective date where appropriate.
